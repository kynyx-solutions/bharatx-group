## Dharampal Singh
**Chief Revenue & Operating Officer**
Photo: `images/leadership/dharampal-singh.webp` (800×1200)
LinkedIn: https://www.linkedin.com/in/dharampal-singh-995aa2228

Dharampal Singh is the Chief Revenue & Operating Officer of BharatX Group. He leads operations, delivery and revenue performance across the Group’s ventures. He brings more than 28 years of experience in automotive manufacturing and multi-site operations, and has led teams of over 1,000 people.

Before joining BharatX Group, he spent over 27 years at Jay Ushin Limited (JPM Group), which he joined in 1999. Most recently he was Plant Head there, overseeing three group plants in Manesar, Bengaluru and Gujarat. Over his career he has worked across product planning, quality, production, stores, warehousing and spare-parts management. He has a record of raising productivity while bringing down overall costs.

Dharampal specialises in operational excellence, including Lean Manufacturing, Six Sigma, Kanban, and quality control and assurance. He is well versed in global standards such as IATF 16949, ISO 14001 and OHSAS 18001. At BharatX Group, he brings this process discipline to scaling operations, strengthening delivery and driving sustainable growth.

He studied at a Government Polytechnic College and is based in Gurugram, Haryana.

**Core expertise:** Multi-site operations · Lean / Six Sigma · Quality systems (IATF 16949, ISO 14001, OHSAS 18001) · Supply chain & warehousing · Cost optimisation · Large-team leadership
