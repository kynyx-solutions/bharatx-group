This is the text from the prototype (bharatx.kynyx.com), reviewed on 24 Oct 2026 for the new bharatxgroup.org site.

**Labels:** ✅ **Keep** (use as is) · ✏️ **Rewrite** (a suggested version is given) · ❌ **Drop** (don't use)

Tone rules (PRD section 4): plain words, short sentences, no "sovereign", "institutional" or "constitutional", and no number without a source.

## 1. Vision, mission and Viksit Bharat @2047 (recommended final)

The prototype mentions Viksit Bharat only inside the founder's quote. The group's vision should **start from** it, and every business should show what it contributes.

### Vision
> **To build world-class Indian businesses that help make India a developed nation by 2047.**

Longer version (About page):
> Aligned with India's national vision of Viksit Bharat @2047, BharatX Group builds world-class businesses in the sectors a developed India depends on — infrastructure, agriculture, manufacturing and technology — and creates value that lasts for generations.

### Mission
> **To find real gaps in India's economy and build, operate and scale the businesses that fill them — creating jobs, raising farmer incomes, making more in India and bringing technology to every business we touch.**

### Our role in Viksit Bharat @2047 (About page section)

| National goal | What BharatX does | Company | How we'll show it (once verified) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| World-class infrastructure | Roads, bridges and civil works | BharatX Infratech | Km built, projects delivered |
| Prosperous farmers (Annadata) | Value addition and exports for Indian produce | BharatX Agro | Growers connected, tonnes exported |
| Make in India and self-reliance | Castors designed and made in India | Casters Global | Units made in India, ISO certifications |
| Jobs and skills for youth (Yuva) | Jobs across the group; AI training | All companies; AIxperts Labs | People employed, people trained |
| Technology-led growth | AI and automation for Indian businesses | AIxperts Labs | Businesses served |
| Entrepreneurship and MSMEs | Building and scaling new ventures | BharatX Ventures | Businesses built or supported |
| Sustainable growth | ISO 14001 manufacturing; environment, waste and recycling programmes | Casters Global; BharatX Labs Foundation | Programmes run, people reached, waste recycled |

The four focus groups usually given for Viksit Bharat (Garib, Yuva, Annadata, Nari, also called "GYAN") come from secondary sources, not an official portal. Check the wording on the official Viksit Bharat site before using these terms.

### Guardrails for using Viksit Bharat
1. **Say "India's national vision of Viksit Bharat @2047", not the Prime Minister's name or photo.** Naming the national goal keeps the site neutral and stays valid under any government. Using the PM's name, photo or the national emblem commercially can also need permission (Emblems and Names (Prevention of Improper Use) Act, 1950).
2. **Don't use government logos**, including the Viksit Bharat logo, without permission. Nothing should suggest government endorsement or partnership.
3. **Contribution, not slogans:** mention it in the vision, in one line on the Home page and in the About section above. Don't repeat it on every page.
4. **Back it with numbers** as they become verified (jobs, farmers, km, units). This is what makes the alignment believable.

## 9. Legal pages

- **Privacy Policy** and **Terms** exist in the prototype (`frontend/src/pages/PrivacyPage.tsx`, `TermsPage.tsx`, dated 25 Sep 2026). ✏️ **Rewrite:** use them only as a starting outline. The new policy must follow the **DPDP Act 2023** (purpose, consent, retention period, rights, a grievance officer's name and contact) and should be reviewed by a lawyer.

## 10. Do not reuse anywhere

- All **newsroom items** (AI compute nodes in 5 states, 22-language models, aerospace-grade casting line, 1,200+ growers).
- The **Leadership page** wording: "Led Like an Institution", "Boardroom Architecture", "Executive Constitution", "Four Operating Doctrines".
- The **Innovation page** (quantum-resilient security, agentic swarms, sovereign LLMs) until BharatX Labs is operating.
- "Chairman's Statement", "Annual General Assembly", "Official Public Disclosure".
- Footer credit "Made with ♥ by KYNYX" (decide whether the agency credit stays).
